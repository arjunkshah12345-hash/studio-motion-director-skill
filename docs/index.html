Six production gates

No jumping from a prompt to a polished-seeming video. Each gate must pass before the next.

1

Product truth & brief

Inspect the real product; capture audience, promise, proof, CTA, brand tokens. Flag claims that need sourcing.

2

Concept & script

Three distinct concepts, scored 1–5 on clarity, originality, demonstrability, fit, feasibility. Pick one.

3

Styleframes

Freeze one coherent design language as static posters before anything moves.

4

Storyboard & animatic

Every shot's timing, focal point, entry/exit, transition motivation, hold, sound cue. Check it muted.

5

Animation & sound

Frame-accurate motion with motivated continuity; sound that reinforces edits, not masks them.

6

Critique & deliver

Contact sheet at every shot boundary, scored on a 12-point rubric. Fix blockers, then export.

"If the brand were removed, does the film still have a distinctive idea? Is it excellent with the sound off?"

The skill's self-check — revise until the answer is yes.
