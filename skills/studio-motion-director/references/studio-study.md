# Studio study — who sets the bar, and what to take (principles, not artwork)

Study a reel from each before concepting; borrow *approaches*, never proprietary frames or footage.
SaaS-tier reference points you can watch freely: **Linear, Vercel, Raycast, Stripe, Arc** launch films.

## Studios
- **Buck** — enterprise standard. No single look by design; constants are **storytelling, playfulness,
  range**. Design-driven, cross-functional teams; process is *think & wonder → play & explore → make &
  improve*; they often **3D-previs timing before committing to the final medium**. *Take:* design the
  frames first; let the idea choose the technique.
- **Giant Ant** — narrative/emotion first. Start from *who are we talking to, and what do we want them to
  feel?* Handmade, character-driven, often lineless; their DOXA trailers are "one shot, one object, solid
  background" that **build suspense to a reveal with anticipation + sound**. *Take:* one subject per shot;
  earn the reveal.
- **Ordinary Folk** (Jorge Canedo) — craft-led, cinematic, geometric. *Take:* **keyframe detail locked
  to audio**, simple shapes.
- **Oddfellows** — tasteful, characterful, design-forward. *Take:* every still should look like a poster.
- **ManvsMachine** — where **motion meets graphic design + branding**; bold type, 3D, **material
  realism**. *Take:* type as a hero object; real materials, light, depth.
- **Territory Studio** — film UI/FUI (Guardians, Ex Machina, Blade Runner 2049). *Take:* the reference
  when your piece shows an app/product UI — legible, purposeful interface motion.
- **Vucko** (Andrew Vucko) — rhythm and restraint; near-invisible transitions; a different color per
  shot. *Take:* restraint reads as expensive.

## Designers (one lesson each)
- **Jorge Canedo / jr.canest** — keyframes locked to sound; teaches the 10 motion principles (Disney's 12
  consolidated).
- **Andrew Vucko** — make transitions disappear.
- **Ash Thorp** — title/UI design pedigree; type + design systems carry a sequence as much as motion.
- **Emanuele Colombo** — loops you can't look away from: simple shapes, bright contrast, unexpected
  transitions.
- **Markus Magnusson** — the satisfying character loop: simple style, impeccable timing, seamless loops.

Sources: Motion Array studio spotlights (Buck / Giant Ant / ManvsMachine), School of Motion (Ryan Honey),
