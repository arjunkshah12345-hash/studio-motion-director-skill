# Quality rubric — Gate 6 scoring

Score each dimension 1–5 (5 = studio-grade). Anything scored ≤2 is a **blocker**: fix and rescore before
calling the film final. Target: no blockers, average ≥ 4.

| # | Dimension | 5 = studio-grade | 1 = cheap |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Distinct idea** | A specific visual idea survives even if the logo is removed | Generic "premium/cinematic/futuristic" with no idea |
| 2 | **Comprehension** | A stranger can say what the product does after one watch | Confusing; needs narration to parse |
| 3 | **Timing & easing** | Custom curves, front-loaded, overshoot on snaps, consistent per element | Linear / default easing; uniform robotic motion |
| 4 | **Holds & pacing** | Every reveal holds; nothing moves into the next cut | Perpetual motion; copy still moving at the cut |
| 5 | **Staging / hierarchy** | One hero move per beat; eye always knows where to look | Everything animates at once |
| 6 | **Type & layout** | 2 weights + 1 accent; grid, optical alignment, scale contrast; legible as a thumbnail | Chaotic type; tiny/too-fast copy |
| 7 | **Color & depth** | 2–3 inks, value-driven depth, parallax/rack-focus where it helps | 6+ colors, flat, no depth |
| 8 | **Continuity** | Motivated handoffs (shape/position/color/semantic); transitions feel inevitable | Random/near-duplicate transitions |
| 9 | **Product truth** | Real UI / authentic behavior; claims sourced | Invented UI states or unverified claims |
| 10 | **Sound design** | Transients on the frame; drop on the biggest reveal; good on laptop speakers | Silent, or SFX that mask weak motion / off the beat |
| 11 | **Signature moment** | 1–2 memorable beats given room to register | Uninterrupted spectacle, nothing lands |
| 12 | **Finish & delivery** | No cropped text / strobing / drift; correct fps, res, codec, aspect; poster frame | Spelling errors, clipped text, abrupt end |

## Must-pass muted test
Watch with sound off: the story and product value must still read. If it only works with audio, the
picture isn't finished.

## Contact-sheet checklist (per shot boundary)
spelling · factual fidelity · cropped text · contrast (WCAG AA for key text) · strobing/flicker ·
awkward easing · duplicate transitions · element drift · unreadable UI · quiet/overloaded sound ·
abrupt ending.
